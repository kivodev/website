Every app signs in to your own Kivo server. On the same network as the server, the apps find it by themselves; otherwise, enter its address, such as `http://your-server:8484`. Watch progress is stored on the server, so you can stop in one app and resume in another.

## Available now

### Web

Built into the server. Open `http://your-server:8484` in any modern browser on a computer, phone or tablet.
