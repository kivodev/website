Kivo is a self-hosted media server for movies, TV shows, home videos and audiobooks. You point it at your folders; it identifies what's there, fetches artwork and details, and streams to apps on your phone, tablet, TV and browser, at home or away.

Kivo is pre-1.0. It works today, but expect rough edges, and expect the API and settings to change between releases until 1.0.
