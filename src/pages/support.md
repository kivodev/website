## Email us

Write to [support@kivo.watch](mailto:support@kivo.watch) with questions about the Kivo apps or the server. Tell us which app and device you're using, its version, and what happened.

## Report a bug or ask for a feature

Kivo is open source. Search the [open issues on GitHub](https://github.com/kivodev/kivo/issues), and open a new one if yours isn't there.

## Common questions

- **The app can't find my server.** Make sure your device and the server are on the same network, or enter the server's address by hand, such as `http://your-server:8484`. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/).
- **I want to watch away from home.** See [Remote access](/docs/remote-access/).
- **I forgot my password.** Ask the person who runs your server, or see [Reset a password](/docs/reset-password/) if that's you.
- **I want my account deleted.** Accounts live on the Kivo server you sign in to, so its admin can delete yours.

More guides are in the [docs](/docs/).

## Privacy

Read the [privacy policy](/privacy/) to see what Kivo collects. In short, we don't collect your personal information.
