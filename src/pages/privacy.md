Kivo is a self-hosted media server. You, or someone you trust, runs the server, and the Kivo apps connect only to that server. This policy covers the Kivo apps, the Kivo server software and this website, all made by Kivo Labs LLC ("we").

The short version: we don't collect your personal information. There are no Kivo accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your media and your account

Your libraries, your account on the server, your watch progress and history all live on the Kivo server you sign in to. So are the server's logs, including error reports the apps send it when something breaks. They are controlled by whoever runs that server, not by us. We can't see or access them.

To change or delete your account or your watch history, ask the person who runs your server. A server's admin can delete any account on it.

## What stays on your device

The apps keep a few things on your device so they work: the server's address, a sign-in token, your settings, and any videos or audiobooks you download for offline playback. None of it is sent to us. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions the apps ask for

- **Local network**, to find your Kivo server and Cast devices on your network.
- **Camera**, only to scan a QR code to sign in or find your server. The image never leaves your device and isn't saved.
- **Notifications**, on Android, to show download and playback progress.

## Casting

When you cast to a Chromecast or Google TV, the app uses Google Cast, and the TV loads Kivo's player from cast.kivo.watch together with Google's Cast software. Your server sends the media and a short-lived sign-in token straight to the TV. Google's handling of Cast data is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What the server contacts

If you run a Kivo server, it reaches out to a few outside services on your behalf:

- **Metadata and artwork** providers, such as TMDB and Audible, to identify your media. They receive titles and similar details, not who you are.
- **OpenSubtitles**, only if you turn on subtitle downloads with your own OpenSubtitles account.
- **GitHub**, to check for a newer Kivo release and to fetch a public list used to match anime. You can turn the update check off in the server's settings.

These requests come from your server, never from us.

## App stores

Apple, Google, Amazon and Samsung run their own stores and handle your purchase and download data under their own policies. If you've agreed on your device to share diagnostics with app developers, the store may pass anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to us. We use them only to fix bugs.

## This website

kivo.watch is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Emailing us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. Ask and we'll delete them.

## Children

Kivo isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## We don't sell or share data

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
